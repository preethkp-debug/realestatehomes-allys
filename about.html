Our Story

A decade of finding extraordinary homes

Real Estate Homes was founded on a simple idea — that buying a home in the UAE shouldn't feel like buying a contract. It should feel like being introduced.

For more than a decade, we've worked with international families, GCC investors, and resident professionals to find the right address — not just on paper, but for the way they actually live.

Today our team operates across the seven Emirates, with offices in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and an off-market network that gives our clients access to homes most agencies will never list.

How We Work

Discreet, considered, unhurried

The best property decisions are made slowly. We work with a small number of clients at any time — that's how we can do the research, walk every shortlisted unit, and stay with you through to handover and beyond.

Every brief begins with a conversation about life — not square footage. Where you spend your time. Where the children go to school. How often you're in the country. The home recommendation follows from the answer.

Most of our business comes from referrals. That tells us we're doing this part right.
